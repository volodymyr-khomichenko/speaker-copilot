Privacy

Without a login, your talks stay only in this browser. They are not uploaded.

If you sign in with Google, your talks are stored as JSON in Supabase, tied to your Google account email. That copy is used only to sync your talks across your devices.

You can delete the cloud copy any time with “Delete my cloud data” in the app. The talks on this device stay.
